# Background Location Justification

Use this content for Google Play permission declaration, Apple review notes, and support/legal explanations.

## Reviewer Explanation

Analogue IOT requires background location access to provide GPS vehicle tracking and fleet monitoring features for authorized users. Background location allows the app to continue live trip tracking, route history, geofence alerts, and safety monitoring when the app is closed or not actively in use.

Without background location, users may miss important tracking updates, geofence entry/exit events, route records, and safety alerts for vehicles connected to their account.

Location data is used only for app functionality, including live vehicle position, route history, trip replay, reports, geofences, and safety monitoring. It is not sold or used for advertising tracking.

## In-App Disclosure Text

Current app disclosure:

Analogue IOT collects location data from this device to show live vehicle position, trip tracking, route history, geofence alerts, and safety monitoring.

Location may be collected even when the app is closed or not in use so tracking and alerts can continue in the background.

Your location data is used only for GPS tracking features inside this app and is shared with your account for monitoring your vehicles.

## Recommended Store Permission Text

Analogue IOT collects location data to enable live vehicle position, trip tracking, route history, geofence alerts, and safety monitoring, even when the app is closed or not in use.

## Reviewer Demo Steps

1. Log in with the provided reviewer account.
2. Open Monitoring to view live vehicle status and map location.
3. Open Tracks to view route/trip history.
4. Open Zones/Geofences to create or view geofence areas.
5. Open Alerts to view speed, SOS, geofence, and parameter notifications.

## Important Submission Notes

- The disclosure must appear before requesting background location permission.
- The Privacy Policy must mention background location.
- App Store Connect App Privacy must declare precise/coarse location.
- Play Console Data Safety must declare precise/coarse location.
- If background location is not needed for the first release, disable background location to reduce review risk.
