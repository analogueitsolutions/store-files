# Analogue IOT Account Deletion Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

**App:** Analogue IOT — Android and iOS (`com.aits.analogueiot`)  
**Company:** Analogue IT Solutions

## How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Analogue IOT account and associated personal data, email [development.analogue@gmail.com](mailto:development.analogue@gmail.com). You can submit a request without signing in or reinstalling the app.

Include:

- Subject: **Analogue IOT account deletion request**
- Your registered email address or phone number and, if available, your account ID.
- A clear request to delete your account and associated personal data.

Do not send passwords, OTPs, or other login credentials.

## Verification and Processing

We verify account ownership before processing a deletion request to prevent unauthorized deletion. If you no longer have access to your registered email address or phone number, explain this in your request so support can advise on verification.

Support will explain the scope of deletion, any records that must be retained, and the expected completion time. Contact the same email address for progress updates and confirmation of completion. Submitting a request does not immediately delete your account.

## Data Covered by Your Request

The request covers your account and associated personal data held by us, including, where applicable:

- Profile details, such as your name, email address, and phone number.
- Account identifiers and authentication records.
- Personal preferences, notification settings, and account-specific configurations.
- Location and trip history, reports, geofences, alerts, and vehicle/device associations linked to your account.

For organization-managed or shared fleet accounts, support will clarify which records belong to your personal account and which are controlled by the organization. Deleting your user account does not automatically delete another user's account or shared fleet records the organization is entitled to maintain.

## Data Retention

Some records may be retained where necessary to comply with legal obligations, maintain security, resolve disputes, or fulfill contractual obligations. Support will explain any applicable retention reason and period when handling your request, including how deletion affects backups. Retained information is subject to the protections described in our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

## What Happens After Deletion

- You lose access to the deleted account and its associated features and data.
- Permanently deleted data cannot be restored. Save any reports you need before deletion is completed.
- Account deletion does not itself cancel a separate GPS device, fleet service, or organization contract. Contact support about any associated service arrangements.

Uninstalling the app, logging out, or disabling location permissions does not delete your account. To request deletion of specific data without deleting your account, contact support and identify the data concerned.
