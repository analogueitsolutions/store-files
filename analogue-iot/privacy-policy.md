# Privacy Policy for Analogue IOT

Effective date: June 26, 2026  
Last updated: June 26, 2026

Analogue IOT is provided by Analogue IT Solutions ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the Analogue IOT mobile application and related services.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, do not use the app.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information needed to provide GPS vehicle tracking, fleet monitoring, alerts, reports, and account services.

### Account Information

We may collect:

- Name
- Email address
- Login credentials or authentication tokens
- Account role and access permissions
- Organization or fleet account details

### Location and Vehicle Tracking Data

The app and connected GPS devices may collect or display:

- Live GPS location
- Latitude and longitude
- Route history
- Trip start and end locations
- Movement, idle, offline, and inactive status
- Speed and distance
- Geofence entry and exit activity
- SOS, speed, parameter, and safety alerts
- Device and vehicle identifiers

Location data may be collected or processed in the background when this is required for live trip tracking, route history, geofence alerts, and safety monitoring.

### Device and App Information

We may collect:

- App version
- Device installation identifier
- Device type and operating system
- Crash, diagnostic, and performance information
- Notification and app settings

### Stored App Data

The app may store account information, settings, authentication tokens, and saved accounts on your device using secure storage and local app storage. Sensitive credentials are stored using operating-system protected storage when supported.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Provide live vehicle tracking and monitoring
- Display vehicles, devices, locations, routes, and trip history
- Create and manage geofences
- Send or display alerts and notifications
- Generate reports and exports
- Secure user accounts and sessions
- Remember app settings and preferences
- Provide customer support
- Maintain, debug, and improve app performance
- Detect unauthorized access, misuse, or service issues

## 3. Background Location Use

Analogue IOT may request background location access so that tracking, route history, geofence alerts, and safety monitoring can continue when the app is closed or not actively in use.

The app shows a background location disclosure before requesting this permission. You can disable location access at any time from your device settings, but some tracking and alert features may stop working correctly.

## 4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information only when necessary:

- With authorized users in the same account or organization
- With backend service providers that operate the tracking platform
- With map, hosting, analytics, crash reporting, notification, or infrastructure providers
- To comply with law, legal process, or government requests
- To protect rights, safety, security, and prevent misuse
- During a business transfer such as merger, acquisition, or restructuring

Location and vehicle data is shared only as needed to provide tracking and monitoring services to authorized account users.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services such as:

- Google Maps or map services
- Backend hosting and API services
- Push notification services
- App store services
- Crash, diagnostic, or analytics tools, if enabled

These third parties process information according to their own privacy policies.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain security.

We retain account information, GPS history, reports, geofences, settings, and service logs for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain account records, support fleet operations, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect security.

Users or account administrators may request deletion of eligible data by contacting us at development.analogue@gmail.com, subject to legal, security, contractual, and operational requirements.

## 7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information, including secure credential storage on the device and protected communication with backend services.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. User Choices and Controls

You can:

- Disable location permission in device settings
- Disable background location permission in device settings
- Manage notification preferences in the app or device settings
- Log out of the app
- Request access, correction, or deletion of eligible personal information

Disabling permissions may limit or disable tracking, geofence, alert, and report features.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Analogue IOT is intended for business, fleet, vehicle owner, and authorized tracking users. It is not intended for children under 13 years of age, or the minimum age required by local law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 10. International Transfers

Information may be processed and stored in countries other than your country of residence. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted through the app, website, or other appropriate notice. The updated policy will show the latest effective date.
