Where an employer or fleet operator provides your account, that organization determines which vehicles and fleet records you may access. Its monitoring practices may also be subject to its own privacy notices. This does not limit our responsibilities for information we process.

## 2. Information we process

### Account and support information

We process account information supplied during registration or account management, including your name, email address, phone number where supplied, and authentication information. We use this information to create and administer accounts, authenticate users, manage authorized access, and respond to account-related requests.

When you contact us, we process the contact details, messages, and supporting information you provide to respond to your request. Do not send passwords or one-time authentication codes to support.

### Vehicle and fleet information

The service processes information associated with vehicles and GPS devices linked to your account, including vehicle or device identifiers, GPS positions and timestamps, device status, trip and route history, geofences, alerts, reports, and configuration settings.

We use this information to provide vehicle monitoring, route history, reporting, and geofence or device alerts. Vehicle location may identify or relate to a driver or another person. Fleet operators are responsible for ensuring that their monitoring activities are authorized and that affected individuals receive appropriate notices.

### Phone or tablet location

With your permission, Analogue IOT uses your phone or tablet’s location while you use location-assisted geofence selection or creation. Depending on your device settings, this location may be precise or approximate. Geofence coordinates and settings you save form part of your fleet configuration.

The app is configured for foreground location access, with background phone location disabled on Android and iOS. Separate vehicle GPS trackers may continue reporting vehicle locations to the fleet service when the mobile app is closed. Disabling the app’s location permission does not disable those independent trackers or delete existing fleet records.

### Notifications

When you enable push notifications, notification tokens and relevant delivery settings are used to route alerts to your device. Notification delivery services process the information needed to deliver those messages. You can manage notification permissions through device settings. Notifications may appear on your lock screen according to your settings.

### Biometric authentication and local storage

If you use biometric authentication, your device’s operating system performs the Face ID or fingerprint check. The authentication mechanism provides the app with the result; it does not provide your facial image, fingerprint, or biometric template.

The app uses local storage for account access and preferences and includes secure storage functionality for sensitive local authentication information. Local authentication and secure storage are distinct from the account and fleet records held by the service.

## 3. How we use information

We use information for the following purposes:

- Providing account access and the vehicle-tracking features you use.
- Displaying vehicle locations, trip history, reports, and geofences.
- Managing device configurations, account settings, and authorized fleet access.
- Delivering enabled alerts and necessary service communications.
- Responding to support, privacy, and deletion requests.
- Protecting accounts and addressing misuse or security issues.
- Meeting applicable legal obligations.

Where consent is required for particular processing, we request it. This policy does not itself provide blanket consent to unrelated uses of personal information.

## 4. Permissions and choices

| Access | Purpose | Your control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foreground location | Use your current location when selecting or creating a geofence | Grant, deny, or revoke location access in device settings |
| Notifications | Display enabled alerts | Enable or disable notifications in device settings |
| Face ID or supported biometrics | Authenticate locally on your device | Control biometric access through the available app and device settings |

Denying a permission may prevent the feature that depends on it from working. Revoking a permission does not automatically delete information already stored by the service.

## 5. When information is disclosed

Information may be disclosed to the following recipients as needed for the purposes described in this policy:

- **Authorized fleet users and administrators:** Relevant vehicle information, locations, reports, and settings are accessible according to account permissions.
- **Service providers:** Providers supporting hosting, application infrastructure, maps, notifications, software-update delivery, and support process the information necessary for their services. Providers handling personal information on our behalf must use it for authorized purposes and protect it consistently with this policy and applicable requirements.
- **External navigation services:** When you choose to open a location in another map or navigation application, the information passed to that application is handled under its provider’s privacy policy.
- **Legal and security recipients:** Information may be disclosed where required by law or where necessary and lawful to respond to valid legal process, address fraud, or protect rights and safety.

## 6. Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is processed, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Account information is retained to administer your account and handle related requests. Retention of vehicle locations, trips, reports, geofences, and alerts depends on the service arrangement and the fleet records maintained for that account. Support records are retained as needed to handle requests and related disputes. Information that must be retained for a legal obligation is limited to the relevant records and period.

When handling a deletion request, we explain the records covered, any applicable retention exception, the expected completion time, and how the request affects backups. Contact us for information about retention applicable to your account or fleet service.

## 7. Account and data deletion

To request deletion of your account or associated personal information, email [development.analogue@gmail.com](mailto:development.analogue@gmail.com) with the subject “Analogue IOT account deletion request.” Identify your account using your registered email address or account ID. Do not include passwords or authentication codes.

We may take proportionate steps to verify ownership before acting on a request. We will explain the scope of deletion and any information that must lawfully be retained.

For organization-managed accounts, some shared fleet records may be controlled by your organization. Deleting your individual account does not automatically delete another user’s account or shared records the organization is entitled to maintain. We will explain the relevant responsibilities when handling your request.

Uninstalling the app, logging out, or disabling permissions does not delete your account. Account deletion does not itself cancel a separate vehicle-tracking or fleet-service contract.

## 8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or loss. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your login credentials and device access, and contact us if you suspect unauthorized use of your account.

## 9. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your information, restrict or object to certain processing, or withdraw consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Contact us using the details below to exercise a right or raise a privacy complaint. We may request information reasonably necessary to verify your identity and locate the relevant records. We will handle requests within applicable legal time limits. Where your organization controls the requested fleet records, we will explain how to direct your request. You may also have the right to complain to the relevant authority.

## 10. Children

Analogue IOT is intended for authorized vehicle owners and fleet users and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has supplied personal information without the necessary authorization, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the service or applicable requirements. The revised policy will identify its latest update date. We will provide additional notice or request consent where required for material changes.
