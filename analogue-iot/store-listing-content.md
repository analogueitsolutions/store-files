## Long Description

Analogue IOT is a GPS vehicle tracking and fleet monitoring app designed for users who need reliable visibility into their vehicles and connected tracking devices.

The app helps you view live vehicle locations on a map, check online and offline device status, replay routes, review trip history, create geofences, and receive important alerts for vehicle movement, speed, SOS, and geofence activity.

Key features:

- Live GPS vehicle monitoring on an interactive map
- Vehicle status summary for online, offline, moving, idle, and inactive units
- Route replay and trip history
- Geofence creation and geofence entry/exit alerts
- Speed, SOS, parameter, and safety alerts
- Reports and export support
- Vehicle groups and account switching
- Map, notification, unit, and interface settings
- Secure login with protected credential storage
- Optional device lock/biometric app access

Analogue IOT is intended for authorized users with an active tracking account and registered GPS devices. Location data is used to provide tracking, route history, geofence alerts, safety monitoring, and reports for vehicles linked to the user's account.
