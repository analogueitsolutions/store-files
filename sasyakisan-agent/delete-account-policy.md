Account ownership needs to be verified before personal data is deleted. Contact support for the verification steps and current processing status. If you cannot access your registered mobile number, explain that in your request. Never send an OTP, password or payment PIN by email.

Ask support for the expected completion date and written confirmation of completion. Sending a request does not mean deletion has already finished. If you wish to withdraw a request, contact support promptly; recovery after deletion should not be assumed.

## Data covered

The deletion process must cover the account and associated personal data, including your registered mobile number, profile details, account identifiers, attendance location/selfies, uploaded evidence, task submissions, support records and notification-token associations, in accordance with the no-retention policy below.

For pending orders, service requests or assigned work, identify any relevant reference when contacting support. Account deletion is not itself a refund or cancellation request.

Copies independently held by other users or external organizations are outside our direct control. Ask support how your request affects such records and any related account in the other Sasyakisan application.

## No retention after completed deletion

Our policy is to retain **no personal data after account deletion is completed**. No post-deletion retention exception is specified. Account-associated personal data in our systems, logs and backups under our control is within the scope of this policy.

## What changes after deletion

You will no longer be able to use the deleted account.

This request identifies the **Sasyakisan Agent** account.

Uninstalling the app, signing out or disabling notifications does not delete the account. If you only want certain data removed, contact [support@sasyakisan.com](mailto:support@sasyakisan.com) and specify the data. Support can explain the options for your request.
