## Mobile number and account verification

We collect your mobile number to identify your account, send a one-time password (OTP), verify sign-in and manage account access. The app sends the number to our authentication service and associates it with your account. Mobile-number verification is required for account-based features. Your number is not merely stored on your phone.

Other information is collected when you register or use the features described below.

## Information handled by the app

The information involved depends on the features you use and, where applicable, your assigned role.

| Category | Information | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Account and profile | Name, mobile number, user/role identifiers, an uploaded profile photo, address, village, mandal, district, state and PIN code | Register, authenticate and manage agent access |
| Registration and field information | Land type/size, assigned-area details and information submitted about farms, buyers or facilities | Coordinate authorized field work |
| Attendance | Check-in/out times, coordinates, address and attendance selfies | Record attendance and enable authorized supervision |
| Tasks and submissions | Assignments, status changes, evidence images, supporting documents, assessment notes, eligibility recommendations, approvals and uploaded photo/video content | Perform, review and document field work |
| Support | Information you provide with a support request | Investigate and respond to issues |
| Notification identifiers | Firebase installation/registration identifiers and, on iOS, APNs tokens | Deliver account and operational notifications |
| Technical information | SDK/device/app metadata and request information | Operate connected services |

Task submissions can include information about farmers, buyers or other individuals. Insurance evaluation workflows submit assessment notes, eligibility recommendations, evidence images and supporting documents. Content submissions include uploaded images or video, a title, category, description and source information. Provide only information you are authorized to submit.

## Permissions and choices

Location access supports location-related workflows including attendance. Camera and selected-photo access support images you choose to capture or upload.

You can manage device permissions and notification settings in Android or iOS settings. Some features may not work without the information they require. Turning off notification display does not necessarily stop Firebase installation-identifier processing. Uninstalling the app does not delete your server account.

Registration requires a mobile number, name and location/address fields. Attendance submissions include a selfie and precise location. Initial task-evidence submissions require notes and images; insurance evaluation also requires supporting documents. Profile photo upload is conditional in the registration request.

## How information is used

Information supports account access, requested services, submitted content, notifications, support and authorized field operations.

## Who can receive information

Authorized main agents and administrators may access attendance, assignments, submissions and reports according to their role. Content submitted for publication may become visible in Sasyakisan after review.

Connected services include Firebase for push notifications and OpenStreetMap map tiles. Opening an external navigation app passes the selected destination to that app. Hosting, storage and operating-system geocoding providers also process the requests needed for their features.

**We do not sell personal data.** Service-related processing and disclosures described above are distinct from selling personal data.

## Connected services and security

The app sends information to connected services to carry out the features described above. Contact support for questions about storage or provider processing. No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee complete security.

## Retention

Account information and information submitted through the app are used to provide services while your account is active. Our policy is to retain **no personal data after account deletion is completed**. This includes account-associated personal data in our systems, logs and backups under our control.

## Your choices and requests

For questions or requests concerning access, correction or deletion, use [Support](support.md). We may need to verify that a request relates to your account.

See the [Account Deletion Policy](delete-account-policy.md) for the request route, deletion scope and no-retention policy. Deleting the app from your phone is different from deleting your account.

## Preparing a privacy request

Identify the app and explain whether you are asking about access, correction or deletion of your information. For a correction, describe the information that needs changing. Include only the details needed to explain the request, and use a verified private contact channel for account identifiers. Do not include passwords, OTPs or payment PINs.

## Children and intended audience

This app is intended for authorized Sasyakisan field personnel. You must be **16 years of age or older** to use the app.

If you believe an account belongs to someone under 16, contact support with the information needed to identify the concern. Do not send identity documents unless an appropriate verification process has been explained to you.
