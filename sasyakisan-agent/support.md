## Common troubleshooting

**Why is my mobile number needed?** Your mobile number identifies your account and is used for OTP verification and account access. Other collected information depends on registration and the features you use; see the Privacy Policy.

**OTP not received:** Check the entered mobile number and network signal. Wait for the app’s resend timer, then retry. If the issue continues, contact support with the time of the attempt; do not send the OTP itself.

**Notifications missing:** Enable notifications in device settings and check the internet connection. Some messages appear only when your account or assigned activity qualifies for them. Contact support if expected updates remain missing.

**Location or photo features not working:** Check the requested app permissions in device settings. Location-dependent attendance features may need location access. Do not upload unrelated photos or documents.

**Upload failed:** Check connectivity, retry within the app and retain your original file until submission succeeds. Include the task or submission reference when reporting an issue.

**Role or assignment incorrect:** Contact support with your role and task reference. Do not use another agent’s login to access work.
